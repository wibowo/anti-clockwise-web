Side A
The party playlist game, powered by Apple Music
Turn any get-together into a mixtape. The host plays the party from their iPhone through a speaker, guests join from their phone's browser, and everyone fills hidden song slots under prompts like "Favorite song from high school". Nobody knows whose pick is whose until it plays.
◆Secret songs — every pick stays hidden until it hits the speaker, then all phones reveal it at once.
◆Nothing for guests to install — scan a QR code or type a 4-letter code and you're in.
◆Like, comment, vote to skip — react together, then see who won the night on the leaderboard.
◆Keep the night — save every song that played as an Apple Music playlist.

Knower of Things
A companion app for Things in Rings
Things in Rings is better when nobody has to be the deck. Knower of Things shuffles for you and lends a second brain when a card refuses to be categorized.
◆Card randomizer — draw a fresh card in one tap, no setup, no repeats.
◆Ask the bot — stuck on a Thing? The bot suggests which ring it might belong in.
◆Table-friendly — big type, quick taps, designed to be passed around.
